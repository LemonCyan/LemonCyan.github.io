Next Batch Selection Proposal for Qt Series #

Based on your currently published articles, which cover Build Systems, Core Logic (Threads/Events), and Dialogs, here are three recommended directions for the next batch of content.

Option 1: The "Deep Core" Batch (Recommended) #

Theme: Filling the remaining gaps in Core Architecture. You have "Events" and "Threads", but you are missing the most famous Qt feature: Signals & Slots, and the other massive architecture piece, Graphics View.

  • 信号槽/信号与槽详解.md - Critical Gap. The essence of Qt.
  • 信号槽/信号槽传递大量数据的效率.md - Performance. Very relevant after "Threads" and "Buffer Pool".
  • 绘图/Qt图形视图框架.md - Architecture. The counterpart to QWidgets for complex scenes.
  • Qt/Qt的socket通信.md - Connectivity. Moving beyond local logic to networked apps.

Option 2: The "Rich UI" Batch #

Theme: Mastering data presentation and styling. You just finished "Dialogs". The natural next step is "Main Window" content, specifically styling and complex widgets like Tables.

  • 控件/样式表.md - Visuals. Making apps look modern (crucial for "de-marketing" but high quality).
  • 控件/表格QTableWidget.md - Data Grid. The most common complex widget.
  • 控件/QTableWidget大量数据处理.md - Optimization. Dealing with the lag of large lists.
  • 绘图/QChart绘图.md - Visualization. Business graphics.

Option 3: The "Industrial & Embedded" Batch #

Theme: Real-world hardware and deployment. If your audience is more focused on embedded systems or industrial control (common for Qt).

  • Qt/Qt串口实现.md - Hardware. Connecting to devices.
  • Qt/Qt Creator 源码学习笔记03，大型项目如何管理工程.md - Engineering. Managing complexity.
  • 数据库/数据库Sqlite3的使用.md - Data. Storing configs and logs.
  • Qt/打包分发应用.md - Delivery. Shipping the binary.

My Recommendation #

Go with Option 1 (Deep Core). Your "Thread Management" and "Event Processing" articles are advanced topics. It feels odd to have those without the deep dive into Signals & Slots and Graphics View. Once the "Hard Core" section is complete, you can move to the "Rich UI" section for the fun stuff.

Shall I proceed with preparing the articles from Option 1?
